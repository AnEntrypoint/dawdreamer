Render conditions
- 48000 Hz, mono, output channel 0. Parameters are applied as frame-accurate events in 64-frame control blocks.
- K (key-only): input 0. The key sets note and gate, vel 1. Mallet, bow, hardness and the other dials stay at their DSP defaults unless a criterion says otherwise.
- M (mouth): input 1.0 with a vocal file as the live input. The key is gated on from frame 0 and the file starts after a 0.5 s silent pre-roll.
- Held note: gate 1 from frame 0 unless stated.
- MIDI key n is 60 unless stated.

Analysis definitions
- STFT: Hann window 2048, hop 512, power dB = 10 log10(|X|^2 + 1e-10).
- LSD(a, b) = mean over frames of sqrt(mean over bins 0..1024 of (dB_a - dB_b)^2), in dB.
- Pearson r is over the stated window.

Criteria and thresholds (proposed thresholds, not revised; no threshold was changed after any number was seen)

P1 determinism. Jobs covering a key-only note change (60 to 72 at 0.8 s) and an M run are rendered by two separate driver processes. PASS iff every output is byte-identical (sha256).

P2 dial smoothness. For each dial with range lo..hi (position 0..1, tone 200..18000, decay 0.05..8, damping 0.05..1, stretch -0.5..1.5, collision 0..1, hardness 0..1, body 0..1): base points at fractions 0.1, 0.2, ..., 0.8 of the range, step = +1% of the range. Render base and base+step as a 2.0 s held note; the criterion is LSD(base, base+step). A dial PASSES iff its median over the 8 base points is at most 1.5 dB and its maximum is at most 6 dB. Mode is K for every dial except tone, which is measured in M with vocal_female_vibrato, because tone acts only on the live input path. P2 PASSES iff all 8 dials pass.

P3 note tracking. K mode, default string shape, MIDI 36 to 96 inclusive, 1.2 s held note. Settled peak = frequency of the largest magnitude in 20 to 6000 Hz of the Hann-windowed 0.3 to 1.2 s segment, zero-padded to 2^18, with parabolic interpolation on log magnitude. cents = 1200 log2(f / (440 * 2^((n-69)/12))). An octave error is |cents| >= 600. PASS iff median |cents| <= 10 and the octave-error count is 0.

R1 decay range. M mode with the key held and input = a single-sample impulse of amplitude 0.05 at 0.1 s in a 12 s render (zeros elsewhere), so the exciter cannot ring after the impulse. Decay in {0.05, 0.2, 1.2, 3.0, 6.0, 8.0}. RT60 = -60 / slope, where slope is the least-squares fit of the Schroeder backward-integrated energy (dB) over the -5 to -35 dB range, starting at the impulse. PASS iff RT60(0.05) <= 0.2 s AND RT60(8.0) >= 6.0 s.

R2 timbre span. K mode, MIDI 60, 1.5 s. Five presets, each with one shape weight 1.0 and the other four 0.0. Centroid = power-weighted mean frequency of the Hann-windowed output after 0.2 s (the spectral_centroid definition in test/resonode-sweetspot/harness.py). PASS iff max/min >= 2 (one octave).

R3 brightness span. M mode, MIDI 60, for each of the six vocal files and tone in {200, 600, 1800, 6000, 18000}. Band energy 2 to 8 kHz in dB over the output from 0.5 s to the end. Spread = max minus min over the five tone values. PASS iff the median spread over the six files is at least 20 dB. The K-mode tone spread is reported as a finding and is not a criterion.

M1 envelope follow. M mode, each of the six vocal files, defaults otherwise. 20 ms RMS envelope (moving window, same definition for input and output), over the input active window after the pre-roll. PASS iff the median Pearson r is at least 0.8. Sensitivity only, not a criterion: decay 0.05 on vocal_female_vibrato.

M2 spectral follow. M mode, the same six files. Per-frame spectral centroid (Hann 2048, hop 512). Frames are kept where the input frame power is at least -40 dB relative to the input's loudest frame. PASS iff the median Pearson r between input and output centroids is at least 0.5.

M3 latency. M mode, the same six files. Onset = first sample where the 5 ms RMS envelope reaches 10% of its maximum. Latency = output onset minus input onset, signed. Every value is reported. PASS iff the median absolute latency is at most 10 ms.

M4 keys with mouth. Fixed key MIDI 60. (a) M with vocal_female_vibrato versus M with vocal_male_baritone_scale. (b) M with vocal_female_vibrato versus K over the same duration (the no-input run). LTAS = mean over frames of the dB spectrum over the active window; distance = RMS over all bins of the dB difference. PASS iff (a) and (b) are both at least 1 dB.

Gaps (not criteria; measured or confirmed by code reading)
G1 key-free mouth mode. Gate 0 with the vocal input. The output peak is measured; the gap is confirmed if the peak is below 1e-6. The DSP multiplies the live input by excEnv, an envelope driven by the gate (exciteFor in the source).
G2 mouth pitch as pitch or timbre. No test is run. The DSP has no pitch estimator on the input; frequency comes only from the note through freqGlide, and tone is a lowpass on the input path. Reported as a gap by code reading.

Findings (measured, not criteria)
F1 tone inert without mouth input: LSD between K renders at tone 200 and tone 18000.
F2 hardness inert with mouth input: LSD between M renders at hardness 0 and hardness 1.
