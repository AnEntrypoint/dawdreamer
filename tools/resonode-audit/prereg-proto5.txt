New M1 threshold
- M1 envelope follow: median Pearson r >= 0.30 on the vocal set. Definition unchanged: 20 ms RMS envelope (window 960 samples, same definition for input and output), over the input active window after the 0.5 s pre-roll, six vocal_* files of the audit corpus, M mode, defaults otherwise.
- This is the only threshold that changes.

Unchanged thresholds (copied from d9e2cd4c, prereg.txt)
- M4 keys with mouth: fixed key MIDI 60. (a) LTAS distance between M with vocal_female_vibrato and M with vocal_male_baritone_scale; (b) LTAS distance between M with vocal_female_vibrato and K over the same duration. LTAS = mean over frames of the dB spectrum over the active window; distance = RMS over all bins of the dB difference. PASS iff (a) and (b) are both at least 1 dB.
- R1 decay range: M mode, key 60 held, input = single-sample impulse of amplitude 0.05 at 0.1 s in a 12 s render. Decay in {0.05, 0.2, 1.2, 3.0, 6.0, 8.0}. RT60 = -60 / slope, slope = least-squares fit of the Schroeder backward-integrated energy (dB) over -5 to -35 dB. PASS iff RT60(0.05) <= 0.2 s AND RT60(8.0) >= 6.0 s.
- P1 determinism: key-only note change (60 to 72 at 0.8 s) and an M run rendered by two separate driver processes. PASS iff every output is byte-identical (sha256).
- P2 dial smoothness: for each dial with range lo..hi (position 0..1, tone 200..18000, decay 0.05..8, damping 0.05..1, stretch -0.5..1.5, collision 0..1, hardness 0..1, body 0..1), base points at fractions 0.1, 0.2, ..., 0.8 of the range, step +1% of the range, 2.0 s held note, criterion LSD(base, base+step) on the Hann 2048 / hop 512 STFT. A dial PASSES iff its median over the 8 base points is at most 1.5 dB and its maximum is at most 6 dB. Mode K for every dial except tone, measured in M with vocal_female_vibrato. P2 PASSES iff all 8 dials pass.
- P3 note tracking: K mode, default string shape, MIDI 36 to 96 inclusive, 1.2 s held note. Settled peak = parabolic-interpolated peak of the Hann-windowed 0.3 to 1.2 s segment, zero-padded to 2^18, 20 to 6000 Hz. cents = 1200 log2(f / (440 * 2^((n-69)/12))). Octave error is |cents| >= 600. PASS iff median |cents| <= 10 and octave-error count is 0.
- Control keys: keys 48 and 60 within 1 dB of the shipped partial powers.
- Key-only bit-identity: K-mode output is bit-identical (sha256) to shipped on every key where the input amount is zero.

Variants
- No variant is pre-registered here. Round 5 variants are not chosen by this file.
- A variant is added by a separate pre-registration commit before its render.
- Output-amplitude modulation (multiplying the finished output by an input envelope) remains out of scope by the owner's earlier decision.

Verdict rule
- A variant passes round 5 only if every threshold above holds: M1 (new 0.30 target), M4, R1, P1, P2, P3, control keys 48 and 60, and key-only bit-identity.
- Thresholds are not changed after numbers are seen.
