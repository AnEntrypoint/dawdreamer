Question A (octave errors)
- Keys: 38, 44, 64 (the v2 errors) and 48, 60 (controls). Length 1.2 s, as in P3.
- Estimator: resonode_audit.settled_peak (Hann over 0.3 to 1.2 s, zero-padded to 2^18, largest magnitude in 20 to 6000 Hz, parabolic interpolation on log magnitude). f_nom = 440 * 2^((n-69)/12). cents_est = 1200 * log2(f_est / f_nom).
- Partial k, k = 1..6: P_k is the largest |X|^2 over bins from 0.94 k f_nom to 1.06 k f_nom in the same spectrum (Hann, 0.3 to 1.2 s, zero-padded to 2^18). The band covers the stretch-jitter range of the string ratios (exponent 1 +/- 0.02). dB_k = 10 log10(P_k / max over j of P_j), j = 1..6.
- k_chosen = the k in 1..6 that minimises |1200 * log2(f_est / (k f_nom))|.
- RULE A: the error is a PEAK-PICKING ARTEFACT iff dB_1 - dB_2 < -12 AND k_chosen = 2. Otherwise it is an INSTRUMENT PROPERTY. Applied as written to keys 38, 44 and 64. Controls 48 and 60 get the same output, labelled control.
- Reproduction: cents_est for keys 38, 44, 48, 60, 64 are compared with the v2 P3 cents for the same keys.

Question B (smoothness steps)
- Dials: position (range 0 to 1) and stretch (range -0.5 to 1.5). Base points at fractions 0.1 to 0.8 of the range, step +1% of the range, 2.0 s held note at MIDI 60. Render base and base+step: 8 pairs per dial.
- Bin-aligned LSD: resonode_audit.lsd exactly (Hann 2048, hop 512, bins 0..1024, mean over frames of the RMS dB difference).
- Smoothed LSD: per frame, each bin's power is replaced by the mean power over bins j with k * 2^(-1/6) <= j <= k * 2^(1/6), always including bin k (so every band has at least one bin). The LSD is then computed on the dB of the smoothed power, same frames and bins.
- Per dial, over the 8 base points: median and maximum of each LSD.
- RULE B: the dial's smoothness FAIL is a METRIC ARTEFACT iff the bin-aligned median > 1.5 dB AND the smoothed median <= 1.5 dB. Otherwise it is REAL. A dial whose bin-aligned median is at or below 1.5 dB has no median FAIL to classify and is reported as NO MEDIAN FAIL.
- Reported next to rule B, not a verdict: the same test on the maximum with 6 dB (bin-aligned max > 6 dB AND smoothed max <= 6 dB gives MAX ARTEFACT).
- Reproduction: the bin-aligned median and maximum per dial are compared with the v2 P2 lines for position and stretch.
